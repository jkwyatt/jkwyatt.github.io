My research uses hydrographic sections, moorings, autonomous platforms
and satellite observations to understand ocean circulation and
water mass variability.

## Greenland–Scotland Ridge exchange

The exchange of warm Atlantic Water and cold, dense overflow water
across the Greenland–Scotland Ridge forms an important part of the
Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation.

My most recent work is focused on constraining the volume, heat and salt 
transport through the Faroe-Shetland Channel. This work involves combining 
satellite altimetry with in situ observations to give a 30 year time series
of changes in transport of the Atlantic Water layer. 

## Southern Ocean satellite hydrography

I have ongoing work with Southern Ocean reconstructions from satellite. 
During my PhD, we developed an updated Satellite Gravest Empirical Mode (SatGEM) 
methodology and with it reconstructed variability of key water masses in the 
Southern Ocean. 

Currently, we are using the SatGEM method with the high resolution SWOT 
satellite to reconstruct vertical velocities in the ocean.
