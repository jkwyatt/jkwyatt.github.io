## I’m an observational oceanographer interested in understanding ocean circulation, water mass variability and climate change. 

I work at the Faroe Marine Research Institute, where my research focuses on the exchange of Atlantic and dense overflow waters across the Greenland–Scotland Ridge.

My current research includes observations of Atlantic Water transport through the Faroe–Shetland Channel, overflow across the Iceland–Faroe Ridge, and the development of satellite and hydrographic methods for reconstructing ocean structure in the Southern Ocean. 

I completed my PhD at the Institute for Marine and Antarctic Studies, in Tasmania, where I focussed on Southern Ocean water mass variability. 

I'm an also a keen trail runner, and general outdoor and vibes enthusiast.
